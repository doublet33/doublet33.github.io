A little about me

Digital leadership, grounded in technical experience.

I’ve spent more than a decade in higher education, progressing from hands-on web developer through senior and lead development roles into digital leadership. I know how websites are built because I’ve built them myself, and I understand the practical details of web development from the inside out.

I’ve led technical teams including web developers and UX designers, as well as non-technical teams across content, social media and CRM. That mix lets me connect strategy with delivery: in planning meetings, I can assess what’s feasible, what can be integrated, how much effort a build is likely to take, and what it may cost.

At Goldsmiths, I progressed from Web Development Manager to Head of Web and Digital Experience. Before that, I managed digital experience at Middlesex University and led web development at the University of Hertfordshire.
